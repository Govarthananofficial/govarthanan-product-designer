Process

How I build
products.

Most designers stop at Figma. I go end-to-end — from business goals to shipped frontend. That's what makes the difference.

01
Understand business goals
I start every engagement by mapping business constraints, KPIs, and stakeholder priorities — before opening Figma. Design that doesn't serve a business goal is decoration.
Stakeholder alignment
02
Research users
Interviews, usage data, and behaviour patterns — to understand what users actually need versus what they say they want. This is where most product decisions get made.
User research
03
Define flows
Information architecture, task flows, and edge case mapping before any visual work begins. A clear flow prevents expensive redesigns downstream.
Information architecture
04
Design systems
Components, tokens, and patterns built for scale — not one-off screens. Reusable systems ship 25% faster and stay consistent across teams and platforms.
Figma design systems
05
Prototype
High-fidelity interactive prototypes that communicate motion, micro-interactions, and state transitions. Stakeholders see the real product — not a static mockup.
Interactive prototyping
06
Build frontend
I implement in Angular, Flutter, and GSAP — so the shipped product matches the design exactly. No handoff gaps, no translation loss, no "the dev changed it" problems.
Angular · Flutter · GSAP
07
Measure outcomes
Engagement rates, task completion, retention, and business impact tracked post-launch. Design accountability doesn't end at handoff — it ends when the metric moves.
Analytics · Impact tracking

Product Thinking

Systems over
surfaces.

Every decision I make is rooted in business context, user reality, and implementation feasibility — not aesthetic preference.

01
Business context before pixels
Every design decision traces back to a business constraint, user need, or product goal. Not aesthetic preference.
02
Design for scale, not for screenshots
Components, tokens, and patterns built to handle edge cases, responsive states, and real data — not Dribbble viewports.
03
Handoff is part of the design
Implementation-ready specs, motion documentation, and developer-aligned delivery.
04
Founder thinking at every level
Product decisions made with cost, velocity, and user retention in mind — not just UX correctness.
05
Motion as functional infrastructure
Animation that communicates state, guides attention, and reduces cognitive load. Packaged and shipped as production tooling.
