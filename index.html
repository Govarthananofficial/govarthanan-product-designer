Product Thinking

surfaces.

Every decision I make is rooted in business context, user reality, and implementation feasibility — not aesthetic preference.

01
Business context before pixels
Every design decision traces back to a business constraint, user need, or product goal. Not aesthetic preference.
02
Design for scale, not for screenshots
Components, tokens, and patterns built to handle edge cases, responsive states, and real data — not Dribbble viewports.

Founder thinking at every level
Product decisions made with cost, velocity, and user retention in mind — not just UX correctness.
